# Build log

What each working session did, decided and got wrong, written for a reader who was not there. Each session's entry is its own file, listed below, oldest first.

**What an entry carries.** Every decision about the build itself, with its reason, and every wrong turn caught, dated. Each line has a source in the private notes. Upkeep of the owner's other work (keeping shared notes tidy, the state of other work, the lists and logs he keeps for himself) is kept private: the entry counts those items in one line, saying how many and why. At each close, a reader that did not do the work checks both ways: every private item is either in the entry or in that count, and every line in the entry has a source in the private notes. This rule starts with the second session; the founding entry was written before it and stands as written.

**Corrections** are added below the text they correct, dated. Nothing above them is edited.

Each entry is written by the session's AI lead at the close and read by the owner. Clock times are marked *(read)* when read from a clock or a commit and *(estimated)* when not. Times are US Central.
