# Build log

What each working session did, decided and got wrong, written for a reader who was not there. Every line has a source in the private notes, and a reader that did not do the work checks the two against each other at each close. Corrections are added below the text they correct, dated; nothing above them is edited.

Clock times are marked *(read)* when read from a clock and *(estimated)* when not.

*The first entry, the founding session of 2026-10-07, is written at its close.*
